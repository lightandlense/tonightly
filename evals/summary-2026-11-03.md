# Tonightly evals: v1 to v3 (2026-11-03)

30 golden conversations in 6 categories, played against Claude Haiku 4.5 on Bedrock, graded by Claude Sonnet 4.6 with `evals/rubric.md`. Every version below was scored by the same final judge (`--rejudge`), so the numbers compare like for like. The case score is the judge's holistic 1 to 5 ("would the person on the couch be happy?").

## What changed

- **v2 prompt:** check facts with get_title before stating them, never ask the same question twice, a crime film is not a comedy, treat "no X" as a filter.
- **v3 prompt:** search again after every correction, never repeat a title, "bittersweet" rules out war, prison and illness stories.
- **Code fixes (the bigger win):**
  1. `discover_titles` got a `max_runtime` filter. Without it, "under 90 minutes" made the model check titles one at a time until it ran out of rounds.
  2. Before the last tool round, the model is told to stop looking things up and answer. This took "I lost my place" replies to zero.
  3. Picks must come from a lookup made this turn, so a correction ("no musicals") can't reuse stale results. Opening an earlier title still works.

Repair got worse with prompt changes alone (v2 3.00). The model ignored "search again" and reused old results. The fix had to be enforced in code, the same way grounding is.

## Checking the judge

Russell hand-scored 10 v1 conversations before seeing the judge's scores.

| Judge version | Exact match | Within 1 point |
|---|---|---|
| First judge (average of 5 criteria) | 1 of 10 | 5 of 10 |
| Final judge (holistic score) | **5 of 10** | **8 of 10** |

The first judge averaged its 5 criteria. A non-answer still got 5s for "grounded" and "broke no rules", so outright failures averaged up to about 3. Other judge bugs found and fixed along the way:
- it took points off for showing fewer than 3 picks
- it claimed markdown that wasn't in the reply
- it couldn't see runtime and season facts, so it marked correct facts as invented
- it invented turns that weren't in a 1-turn case
- once, it answered as the assistant ("Sorry, I lost my place") instead of grading
- it sometimes dropped the closing brace of its JSON

Where Russell and the final judge still differ: on repair-06 (Burlesque after "no musical"), Russell gave partial credit (2 of 3 picks right) and the judge failed the whole conversation for breaking a correction.

## Still open

- **Facts from memory:** memory-02 said "Marple has 3 seasons" without a lookup. The prompt rule alone doesn't hold. Next step is to enforce it in code, for example by rejecting replies that contain season or runtime numbers when no get_title ran that turn.
- **Noise:** scores move about ±0.5 per case between runs at the same settings, and categories have only 4 to 6 cases. Memory dropping from 4.50 to 3.25 is mostly one case flipping. Next step is to run each version 3 times and average.
- **What the judge can't see:** it gets title names, not overviews, so it can't check plot descriptions against the tool data.
